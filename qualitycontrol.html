Quality Control

Quality Control & Precision Testing Specifications

To ensure every printed and packaged product matches client specifications, WASA Printers (Pvt.) Ltd. operates an in-house Quality Control (QC) function staffed by skilled specialists and equipped with advanced measuring instruments. Our laboratory supports production across every stage—from inspecting raw materials such as paper and ink before press runs to verifying color shade accuracy, die-cutting precision, and gloss levels.

Quality monitoring continues seamlessly from initial material selection and press commencement through final product finishing, packaging, and dispatch. Using calibrated tools including spectrophotometers, gloss meters, GSM meters, color matching light boxes, thickness meters, and barcode readers, our team enforces strict compliance and repeatable print consistency across every batch.

Advanced quality testing laboratory

WASA Printers (Pvt.) Ltd. operates a dedicated, state-of-the-art quality control laboratory that safeguards precision across every production stage. From inspecting raw materials like paper and ink prior to press runs to verifying color shade cross-matching, die-cutting accuracy, and gloss intensity, our lab ensures consistent packaging and print quality for every client order.

The team is responsible for ensuring quality at every level i.e. from inspection of raw material being utilize to commencement of produc tion, then finalized product to its packaging and finally until the same is delivered.
