Food Packaging

Takeaway & Food Packaging Solutions

Built for restaurants, cafés, bakeries, caterers, and food retailers, WASA Printers (Pvt.) Ltd. produces food-grade takeaway packaging that protects meals in transit and elevates brand appeal. Our range covers fried chicken and burger boxes, deli and pizza boxes, fries scoops, wrap cases, snack trays, cake and sweet boxes, paper cups and bowls, ice cream cups, cup holders, paper bags, and custom tissue napkins.

Crafted from white paperboard, natural kraft board, and corrugated board with nested shipping for space-saving logistics, each item is available in standard designs or custom-printed sizes. Backed by ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, FSC and Halal certifications, with strict lab quality checks.
