about us

Precision print and packaging at global scale!

Located in the industrial hub of Karachi, Pakistan, WASA Printers (Pvt.) Ltd. is a state-of-the-art ISO 14001:2015, ISO 9001:2015, FSC, and Halal certified printing company with over 23 years of industry expertise. Equipped with comprehensive in-house pre-press, offset and digital press, and post-press finishing facilities, we deliver custom printing and packaging solutions engineered to exact client specifications within strict timelines.

Through our international affiliated companies, we manage local warehousing, invoicing, and buffer stock replenishment—maintaining one to two months of ready inventory in destination markets for uninterrupted supply. Backed by continuous facility upgrades and an experienced leadership team, we build reliable, long-term manufacturing partnerships worldwide.

Commercial Publishing

High-precision books, magazines, manuals, and corporate publications produced with consistent color fidelity.

Product Packaging

Custom folding cartons and structural retail packaging engineered to protect brands and streamline supply.

Point of Sale Display

Impactful counter displays, wobblers, and retail merchandising materials designed to drive shelf visibility.

Takeaway Food Packs

Hygienic, compliant food boxes and takeaway bags built for durability, safety, and dependable replenishment.
